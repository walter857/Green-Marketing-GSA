### FAQ Section Heading

Lawn Aeration Questions

### FAQ 1

Question: What is lawn aeration?

Answer: Aeration opens up compacted soil so air, water and nutrients can reach the roots more easily.

### FAQ 2

Question: When should my lawn be aerated?

Answer: Timing depends on your grass type and your lawn's condition. Tell us about your lawn and we will advise on timing when we quote.

### FAQ 3

Question: Will my lawn look different afterwards?

Answer: You will see the effects of the work on the surface for a short period. Mowing continues as normal.

### FAQ 4

Question: Do I need to do anything before you arrive?

Answer: Mark irrigation heads, invisible fence lines and shallow utilities, and clear toys and furniture from the lawn.

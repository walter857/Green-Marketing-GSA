## 10. FAQ SECTION AND SECOND FORM

### FAQ Section Heading

Aeration And Overseeding Questions

### FAQ 1

Question: Why aerate before overseeding?

Answer: Aeration opens the soil so seed can make contact with it rather than sitting on top of thatch, where it is more likely to dry out.

### FAQ 2

Question: How soon will I see new grass?

Answer: Germination timing depends on grass type, soil temperature, weather and how consistently the lawn is watered. We will set expectations when we quote.

### FAQ 3

Question: How should I water afterwards?

Answer: New seed needs the surface kept consistently moist while it germinates. We will go through this with you.

### FAQ 4

Question: When can I mow again?

Answer: Hold off on the first mow until the new grass is established enough to handle it, and keep the mower height higher for that first cut.

### FAQ 5

Question: Do you cover my area?

Answer: EKG Lawn serves Midlothian, Woodlake, Brandermill and surrounding service areas. Submit your ZIP code or city to confirm Aeration & Overseeding availability for your location.

What Are Permanent Holiday Lights?

Permanent Christmas lights are an innovative lighting solution designed to be installed once and used year-round. Unlike traditional holiday lights that need to be set up and taken down annually, permanent holiday lights are discreetly installed along the eaves of your home and controlled via an app, allowing you to customize colors, patterns, and schedules. This modern approach to holiday lighting saves time, enhances safety, and offers flexibility for every season and occasion.

What Are Permanent Holiday Lights?

Permanent holiday lights (also called permanent Christmas lights or year-round accent lights) are professional-grade LED lighting systems that are installed once along the eaves of your home and then used every day of the year. Unlike traditional string lights you hang and take down annually, EverLights are discreet during the day and controlled from your phone. You can instantly change colors, patterns, and schedules for holidays, birthdays, game days, parties, or just everyday accent lighting.

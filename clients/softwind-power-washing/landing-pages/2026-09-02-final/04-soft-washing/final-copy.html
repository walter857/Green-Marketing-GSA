Soft Washing questions

What is the difference between soft washing and pressure washing?

Soft washing uses low pressure and a cleaning solution. Pressure washing relies on force. Some surfaces need one, some need the other.

Is soft washing better for older siding?

It is often the right choice where high pressure would be too aggressive.

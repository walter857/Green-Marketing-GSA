### Why the order is what it is

**Stage 4 comes after Stage 5 on purpose, and that ordering is not negotiable.** A routing negative
added before its owner ad group is live and verified serving does not route traffic — it deletes it.
Every sibling negative waits until its owner is proven eligible.

**Stage 6 (CPC) comes after the structure and the pages, not before.** Ad Rank is bid × Quality
Score. Cutting the bid while landing-page experience reads BELOW_AVERAGE attacks the one factor that
is already working. Fix the page and the ads first, re-measure, then set the ceiling against
post-structure evidence.

**No CPC number is pre-committed. Not $12, not $15, not $18, not $22.** Every one of those appeared
in an earlier plan or conversation, and none of them survives as an input. The figure is derived
fresh at Stage 6 from the then-current CPC distribution, impression share, Quality Score and
post-landing-page performance.

**Bidding stays on Maximize Clicks** until conversion tracking is verified, qualified conversions
are actually recording, there is enough reliable data, and lead quality can be judged. Switching to
Maximize Conversions because the account needs leads would be bidding toward a signal that does not
yet exist. When it becomes justified it gets its own plan, never combined with consolidation or RSA
changes.

This produces an uncomfortable but important conclusion:

> **Fixing the CPC will *reduce* August spend, not raise it.**

spend number.

## 7. The verdict

**IS $2,500 MONTH-END SPEND REALISTIC WITH THE CURRENT STRUCTURE? — NO.**

**$2,500 IS NOT A DEFENSIBLE QUALIFIED-SEARCH SPEND TARGET FOR THE REMAINING PERIOD.**

| | |
|---|---:|
| Goal | $2,500.00 |
| **Highest defensible month-end spend** (Scenario C) | **$638.49** |
| **Shortfall** | **$1,861.51 — 74.5% of the goal** |
| Impressions/day the goal requires | **238** |
| Impressions/day the maximum defensible plan produces | **47.8** |
| **Share of required demand that exists** | **20%** |

The gap is not a budget problem, a bid problem or an effort problem. **The qualified
search demand does not exist in this footprint.** Across the eight targeted cities the
entire local market generates roughly 800 bottom-of-funnel exterior-washing searches a
month — about 26 a day, every hour of every day combined. The goal needs 238 a day.

Reaching $2,500 in six days would require every one of the following, and each is either
prohibited by Green Marketing policy or unsupported by verified client facts:

- Broad Match — **prohibited without explicit approval**
- Search Partners — **off by default, prohibited**
- Display or Performance Max — **prohibited**
- Equipment, rental, DIY and jobs traffic — the largest untapped volume in the market
  (EQUIPMENT_OR_PRODUCT = 1,310 searches/month, the biggest category found) and
  **worthless to this client**
- Geographic expansion far beyond the eight verified cities — **the service area is
  UNKNOWN and unverified**; expanding it is a client-fact decision, not a technical one
- Bidding well above $33.83 top-of-page — **already overpaying at $28.53**

### The recommended target instead

**Aim for $550–$640 in August**, via Scenario B or C, and judge the month on
**cost per qualified lead**, not on spend. That is roughly **26% of the stated goal**, and
it is the honest number.

Two things worth saying about the shortfall:

1. **Underspending is the correct outcome here.** The account has spent $171.17 and
   produced zero leads. Forcing $2,328 more through a market that holds 800 monthly
   searches would buy equipment shoppers, DIY researchers and out-of-area clicks. The
   money would be gone and the lead count would very likely still be low.
2. **The seasonal window is closing.** Local Keyword Planner history for the top BOFU
   buckets shows **Aug 2025 = 340 → Sep 2025 = 200, a 41% drop**, falling further through
   October. Late August is one of the last strong weeks of the year for this service. That
   is an argument for fixing the constraints *this week*, not for spending more this week.

| Option | Effect | Requires |

The service-area expansion is the only lever that meaningfully changes the demand ceiling,
and it cannot be pulled from this desk. It needs a verified answer to one question:
**which towns does Softwind actually serve for power washing?**

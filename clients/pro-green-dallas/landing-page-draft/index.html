Sprinkler Problems Waste Water and Damage Your Lawn

A single broken head or leaking valve can flood one area, starve another, and quietly raise your water bill for months. Most DFW systems fail in the same handful of places, and most of those failures are a straightforward repair rather than a replacement.

We find the actual fault, show you what is wrong, and give you a free estimate before any work begins.

  • Sprinkler heads that are broken, sunken, blocked or spraying the wrong direction
  • Zones that will not turn on, will not shut off, or run at low pressure
  • Leaking or stuck valves and valve boxes
  • Controller and timer faults that leave zones running or skipped

What We Repair

Sprinkler Repair

Broken, sunken, clogged and misaligned heads, cracked laterals, and coverage problems that leave dry or flooded patches.

Irrigation Repair

Mainline and lateral leaks, pressure loss, wiring faults, and zones that have stopped responding.

Sprinkler Head Repair

Head replacement, nozzle matching, and spray-pattern correction so every zone waters evenly.

Valve Repair

Stuck, leaking and failed valves, solenoid faults, and valve-box access and repair.

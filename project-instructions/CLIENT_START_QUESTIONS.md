# Green Marketing Client Start Questions

Ask the first three questions every time unless the answers already appear in a validated work order for the current client and current task.

## Required before any work

1. What is the exact child account name as shown in Google Ads?
2. What is the 10-digit child customer ID? This must be the client account ID, not Green Marketing's MCC ID.
3. What exact service are we working on?

Then confirm the approved vertical:

- Landscaping.
- Hardscaping.
- Heating and cooling/HVAC.
- Similar local service, with Green Marketing approval.

## Required task definition

4. What should be done: audit, new campaign, campaign revamp, keyword research, ads/assets, landing page, conversion tracking, search-term cleanup, Local Services Ads, lead follow-up, offline conversions, reporting, or a full-system build?
5. Should this be read-only, a draft, create-paused after approval, or enable after QA?

## Ask only when relevant

- Business name and website/final URL.
- Exact service area and exclusions.
- Daily or monthly budget.
- Call-answering hours and after-hours coverage.
- Primary conversion goal and CRM stages.
- Verified offers, licenses, warranties, financing, ratings, review count, and other claims.
- Approved photos, testimonials, phone number, and landing-page template.
- Existing campaigns, keywords, negatives, conversion actions, or landing pages that must be preserved.
- Date range for an audit or search-term review.
- Approval owner for creation, enablement, budgets, conversions, or deletion.

Do not request developer tokens, OAuth secrets, refresh tokens, API keys, webhook secrets, or customer PII in chat.

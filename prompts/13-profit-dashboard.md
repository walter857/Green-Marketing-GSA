# Build a Profit-First Dashboard

Validate the current work order first. Display the exact child account name, child ID, vertical, and service in the dashboard context.

Join Google Ads/LSA spend with CRM and call-tracking outcomes using customer ID, click IDs, lead ID, phone, timestamp, and campaign dimensions.

Required metrics:

- Spend, clicks, calls, forms, leads, qualified leads, booked jobs, sales.
- Revenue, fulfillment cost, gross profit, sales cost, and profit after ad spend.
- CPL, CPQL, CAC, close rate, ROAS, and gross-profit ROAS.
- Speed-to-lead and contact rate.
- Breakdown by campaign, ad group, keyword/search term, service, location, lead type, device, landing page, day, and hour.
- Attribution/match rate and unassigned revenue.

Definitions:

- Gross profit = revenue − fulfillment cost.
- Profit after ads = gross profit − ad spend − attributable sales cost.
- ROAS = revenue ÷ ad spend.
- Gross-profit ROAS = gross profit ÷ ad spend.

Rank recommendations by estimated recoverable dollars, state assumptions, and show data freshness. Never present profit when cost inputs are missing without labeling it as incomplete.

# Local Service Lead Call Playbook

Validate the current work order first. Write the playbook for the exact primary service rather than a generic company script.

Create a short, client-specific playbook for inbound Google Ads and LSA leads.

Include:

- Opening that confirms the request and reduces friction.
- Discovery: problem, property/project, location, urgency, timeline, decision maker, and previous attempts.
- Qualification using the client's real service boundaries and minimum job size.
- Value explanation tied to verified USPs and the caller's problem.
- Clear next step: inspection, estimate, booking, or disqualification.
- Objection handling for price, timing, spouse/partner, comparison shopping, and trust.
- Follow-up schedule and ownership.
- CRM fields and disposition codes.
- Quality scorecard for call review.

Do not use manipulative pressure, false scarcity, invented discounts, or promises operations cannot keep.

# Full Green Marketing Client Build

Run these phases in order and pause at each approval gate:

1. Collect exact child account name, 10-digit child customer ID, exact service, and vertical.
2. Validate the work order against the client profile and fresh read-only account identity.
3. Read-only account and measurement audit.
4. Profit model and success targets.
5. BOFU keyword and focused ad-group architecture.
6. Campaign, two-RSA, asset, and negative-keyword draft.
7. Landing-page build and local QA.
8. Form, call, CRM, click-ID, and conversion verification.
9. Create Google Ads resources paused.
10. Readback QA and Editor/UI review.
11. Separate enablement approval.
12. Speed-to-lead and sales handoff.
13. Data Manager offline conversion validation.
14. Profit dashboard and weekly operating cadence.
15. Search-term/negative review after data accrues.

Never skip the validated work order, client identity, exact service, plan fingerprint, create-paused state, readback QA, or separate enablement approval.

# Generate Two RSAs and Assets per Ad Group

For each approved ad group:

- Confirm the ad group belongs to the exact service in the validated work order.

- Generate exactly two distinct RSAs.
- Use 15 useful headlines when enough verified material exists; never use filler.
- Keep headlines at 30 characters or fewer and descriptions at 90 or fewer.
- Pin only genuinely keyword-relevant variations to Headline 1. Leave other assets unpinned by default.
- Align the primary headline, offer, service, city, final URL, and landing-page H1.
- Use verified USPs only. Do not invent ratings, review counts, years, licenses, warranties, response times, prices, or promotions.
- Avoid unnatural `near me` copy; use `near you` where appropriate.
- Create 4–8 relevant sitelinks, 8–12 callouts, and 1–2 structured-snippet sets only when every claim and URL is verified.
- Do not repeat the same claim across every asset.
- Validate character counts and duplication.

Present copy for approval before adding it to Google Ads. Create approved ads paused.

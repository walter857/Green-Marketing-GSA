# Build a High-Intent Landing Page

Use the client's required template without structural changes when one is supplied.

Require the validated account name, child ID, vertical, and exact service even when the task is draft-only. Ask for the client's approved brand assets before final creative work; use explicit placeholders when they are not supplied.

Default order when no template governs:

1. Top CTA bar before the hero with the verified click-to-call number.
2. Hero with keyword-aligned H1, short value proposition, form CTA, phone CTA, and one verified trust signal.
3. Problem/outcome section.
4. Service scope and surfaces or project types.
5. Before/after or completed-work imagery.
6. Process and response expectations.
7. Three verified, service-relevant reviews.
8. Service area.
9. FAQs that answer buying objections.
10. Final CTA and footer.

Requirements:

- The H1 must naturally match the ad group's canonical intent.
- Keep the form short and accessible.
- Capture GCLID, GBRAID, WBRAID, UTMs, campaign, ad group, keyword, device, referrer, landing page, and a unique lead ID.
- Use click-to-call with separate call tracking.
- Send successful forms to a dedicated thank-you page and deduplicate conversion events.
- Identify each image placement, aspect ratio, subject, and alt-text brief.
- Use only verified services, locations, photos, reviews, phone numbers, and claims.
- Test mobile layout, form validation, phone links, webhook response, thank-you redirect, and tracking before deployment.

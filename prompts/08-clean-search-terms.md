# Review Search Terms and Propose Negatives

Use the selected campaign and requested date range. Pull search terms with cost, clicks, conversions, conversion value, and matched keyword.

Validate the work order first. Judge intent against the exact primary service, approved related services, and actual service area.

For each candidate:

1. Determine whether the searcher could reasonably hire this client for an in-scope service and location.
2. Check whether the term converted or assisted a qualified/sold lead.
3. Check conflicts with positive keywords, brand terms, offers, and existing negatives.
4. Assign KEEP, WATCH, or NEGATIVE with a concise rationale.
5. Recommend Broad, Phrase, or Exact negative match based on how much meaning should be blocked.

Never automatically negative `near me`, `quote`, `price`, `pricing`, `cost`, `best`, `hire`, `local`, or `emergency`.

Show the full verdict table and estimated spend affected. Add only user-approved terms, then read them back and report the final list.

# Create a Local Services Ads Profile Brief

Validate the current work order first. Confirm the exact child account name, 10-digit child customer ID, exact service, and LSA eligibility for the client's location.

Use the verified Google Business Profile, licenses, insurance, service categories, service areas, hours, phone, booking capacity, photos, and review link.

Produce:

- Eligibility and verification checklist.
- Correct business name, category, services, job types, service areas, hours, budget, and lead-routing configuration.
- Photo checklist using real team, vehicle, completed work, and before/after images.
- Review acquisition plan that requests honest reviews from all genuine customers.
- Lead-response SLA and ownership.
- Weekly dispute, booking-rate, response-time, budget, and profitability review.
- CRM/LSA matching fields: phone, timestamp, job type, location, lead type, status, revenue, and gross profit.

Do not claim a ranking formula as guaranteed. Do not alter the Business Profile or LSA account without explicit approval.

# BOFU Keyword Research and SKAG Plan

Use current account data, Search Terms, Keyword Planner when available, service pages, and verified market context.

Start from the exact service in the validated work order and read the matching vertical reference. Do not mix other services into the plan unless they are listed as approved related services.

Rules:

- Include only service-buying intent: service, company, contractor, quote/estimate, emergency, same-day, near me, and service + city when relevant.
- Exclude DIY, jobs, training, retail parts, definitions, inspiration, pictures, and other research intent.
- Default to one canonical intent per ad group with the same keyword in Phrase and Exact.
- Keep close variants together only when ad copy and landing-page intent are truly identical.
- Do not use Broad Match without explicit approval.
- Do not invent search volume or CPC. Label unavailable metrics.
- Identify negative conflicts before recommending keywords.
- Assign one final URL and proposed H1 to each ad group.

Output a table with campaign, ad group, canonical keyword, Phrase, Exact, intent rationale, location, final URL, H1, and negative conflicts. Wait for approval before generating ads or a build plan.

**Is pacing on target?** Yes. The account has used **75.8%** of the monthly budget with seven days

## 10. What Is Too Early to Judge

Be sceptical of anyone — including us — who claims a result from these yet:

1. **Which ad message wins.** Three clean days, 10 clicks, and a result that reversed on day four.
   We need several weeks.
2. **Cost per lead and conversion rate.** Zero measured leads in both comparison weeks. Any
   percentage here would be invented.
3. **Whether the new ads improve Quality Score.** Google's expected click-through rating is still
   "below average" on all six scored keywords. It updates slowly and cannot have responded in four
   days.
4. **Which campaign truly produces better business.** The cost-per-click gap is real; the lead
   evidence is one conversion in 24 days.
5. **Whether the higher "missed due to ad rank" figure matters.** It is the intended cost of the bid
   cap. Whether the trade is worth it depends on leads, which we cannot yet measure.
6. **Individual keyword decisions.** Twenty-six of the 48 keywords drew no impressions at all in
   August. Three days of post-change data is not grounds to pause anything.

## 12. Overall Status

**The account is now built correctly and running efficiently. It is not yet producing measurable
leads, and that is the honest headline.**

The technical work is done and verified: budgets rebalanced, bids capped, schedule corrected,
lead counting cleaned up, competitor traffic blocked, and a proper two-ad test running in every ad
group. The results we can measure are genuinely good — clicks cost 17.7% less than last week, the
account no longer runs out of budget mid-day, and it now appears in roughly two-thirds of the
searches it competes for, up from about a third.

But one measured lead in 24 days on $1,515.72 of spend is not a result anyone should be satisfied
with. The most likely explanation is a mix of two things: the account genuinely needs more time at
its new settings, and the website forms are almost certainly not reporting the leads they do
receive. We can fix the second one, and until we do, we cannot fairly judge the first.

**Status: structurally healthy, financially controlled, measurement not yet trustworthy.** The
priority for the coming week is not more optimization inside Google Ads — it is making the leads
visible.

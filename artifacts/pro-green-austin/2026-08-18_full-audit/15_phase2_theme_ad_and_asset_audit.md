it is why the new ads are outcome-led rather than more feature detail.

Two BELOW_AVERAGE landing-page scores both sit on `paver installation near me` → `pavers-2`. Worth
watching, but two data points on 7 impressions is not enough to act on.

**All 11 previously unvalidated sitelink destinations were fetched in a real browser on 2026-08-19.**
Every one returns HTTP 200, every H1 reads "…in Austin, Round Rock & Cedar Park, TX", gclid survives
to the final URL on all of them, and there are **zero** Dallas or Houston mentions and zero

**The finding that matters:** these main-site pages use form `gform_1`, which **does** capture gclid,
`utm_medium` and `utm_campaign`. The offer pages the ads point at use `gform_3`, which captures
**nothing**. Sitelink traffic is currently better attributed than your primary ad traffic.

So the obvious "make sitelinks match the ads" change is the **wrong** move right now. It is held as
**BLK-002**, blocked until SPEC-001 fixes `gform_3`. Performance across all 12: 4 clicks, $153.72,
zero conversions.

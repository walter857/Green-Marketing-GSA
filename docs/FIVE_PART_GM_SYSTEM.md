# Green Marketing Five-Part System

The transcript's core lesson is that Google Ads performance depends on the full path from search to profit. Green Marketing organizes that path into five connected parts.

## 1. Demand capture

- Check Local Services Ads eligibility and profile quality.
- Build standard Search campaigns around the client's exact service and service area.
- Use Maps-related visibility only when the business and campaign setup support it.
- Research real search demand. Do not ask AI to invent volume or CPC.
- Keep the keyword, ad, and landing page aligned.

## 2. Landing-page conversion

- One exact service intent per page when it materially improves relevance.
- Use a clear H1, short form, click-to-call, verified proof, service area, FAQs, and a specific next step.
- Build variants only when there is enough traffic for a useful test.
- Use client-owned brand references, not copied third-party designs.

## 3. Speed to lead

- Route a new lead to the right person immediately.
- Set the response SLA from the client's real staffing and business hours.
- Track attempts, contact rate, appointment rate, and after-hours handling.
- Do not promise a 60-second response unless the client can deliver it.

## 4. Sales

- Carry the service and source context into the call.
- Qualify consistently.
- Track estimate scheduled, estimate completed, qualified, sold, lost reason, revenue, and gross profit.
- Review call quality and follow-up, not only lead count.

## 5. Profit and feedback

- Capture click IDs and UTMs in the form and CRM.
- Connect ad spend to qualified leads and sold jobs.
- Send consented, deduplicated offline outcomes back through the approved integration.
- Use profit evidence to adjust search terms, budgets, ads, landing pages, and lead handling.

The system is not complete when a campaign is created. It is complete when the selected account and service can be traced from spend to qualified demand, sold revenue, gross profit, and an approved optimization decision.

## Why the live campaign loses impressions to budget but the forecast says $85 doesn't bind

**The live campaign serves only Mon–Fri 08:00–16:00.** Its daily spend against the $85 budget:
- Sept 25: $158.75 (1.87×)
- Sept 28: $168.95 (1.99×)
- Sept 29: $119.24 (1.40×)
- Sept 30: $169.21 (1.99×, provisional)

Spend lands between 08:00 and 14:00. At $27.64 a click, the campaign reaches Google's 2× daily limit by early afternoon on most serving days, and stops serving: that shows up as 16.6% of impressions lost to budget.

**The forecast doesn't model the schedule.** It spreads demand over 24 hours × 31 days and prices clicks at $17.74, not $27.64. That gives $2,110 a month, about $68/day, below $85.

**Both views are consistent.** The binding limit is the compressed schedule multiplied by the real CPC. At $18 the expected ~$14–17 clicks cost about $45–85 per serving day, so the budget-lost share should mostly disappear.

# Hardscaping Reference

Use this reference only after the client confirms `vertical: hardscaping` and names the exact primary service.

## Common service families

- Paver patios and walkways.
- Retaining walls.
- Driveway pavers.
- Outdoor kitchens and fire features.
- Pool decks.
- Steps, stoops, and masonry features.
- Hardscape repair, cleaning, or sealing when actually offered.

Keep each service separate when the ad, proof, project economics, or landing page differs. Do not assume a hardscaper performs general landscaping, concrete, pool construction, or structural engineering.

## High-intent research patterns

- `[service] contractor`
- `[service] installer`
- `[service] company`
- `[service] near me`
- `[service] cost`, quote, or estimate
- `[service] + city`

Price and cost terms can have strong buying intent. Never negative them automatically.

## Likely irrelevant intent to review

- DIY, design ideas, pictures, calculators, free plans, jobs, training, material-only retail, wholesale pavers, equipment rental, and unrelated masonry services.
- Wrong-location searches and services the client does not perform.

## Landing-page proof

Use verified completed-project photos, material choices, site preparation, drainage approach, installation process, service area, and service-specific reviews. Never invent permit handling, engineering, warranties, financing, or material brands.

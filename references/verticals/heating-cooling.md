# Heating and Cooling Reference

Use this reference only after the client confirms `vertical: heating-cooling` and names the exact primary service.

## Common service families

- AC repair, maintenance, and replacement.
- Furnace repair, maintenance, and replacement.
- Heat pump repair and installation.
- Boiler repair or replacement.
- Ductless mini-split service and installation.
- Indoor air quality and ductwork when actually offered.

Separate emergency repair, scheduled repair, maintenance, and replacement when the economics, availability, or landing page differs. Never claim 24/7 service unless the client can answer and dispatch after hours.

## High-intent research patterns

- `AC repair near me`
- `furnace repair + city`
- `HVAC contractor`
- `[equipment] replacement quote`
- `emergency [service]`
- `[brand] repair` only when the client services that brand

## Likely irrelevant intent to review

- DIY troubleshooting, error-code research, manuals, parts, filters, supplies, jobs, apprenticeships, salaries, schools, certifications, and services or equipment brands the client does not support.

## Landing-page proof

Use verified license information, technician photos, service hours, brands serviced, financing, maintenance plans, guarantees, reviews, and call handling. Do not invent certification, same-day arrival, availability, diagnostic fees, rebates, or pricing.

## Measurement notes

Separate calls by meaningful duration and disposition. Push qualified and sold outcomes back when the CRM can connect them to click IDs. Compare repair and replacement economics separately.

# Similar Local-Service Reference

Use this route only when Green Marketing explicitly approves the vertical in the current work order.

The work order must include:

- `vertical: similar-local-service`
- `scope_approval.approved: true`
- The approver's name.
- A reason the service is similar to Green Marketing's landscaping, hardscaping, or heating-cooling work.
- One exact primary service.

The business should normally be a local, appointment- or estimate-driven service with a defined service area, clear lead qualification, and a sales process that can connect leads to outcomes.

Do not use this project for ecommerce, SaaS, legal, medical, financial, employment, education, or unrelated national lead-generation work.

Build a service-specific reference before campaign planning. Identify buying-intent searches, irrelevant research intent, proof requirements, dispatch or scheduling constraints, conversion definitions, sales economics, and regulatory claims that require verification.

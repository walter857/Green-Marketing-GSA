# Landscaping Reference

Use this reference only after the client confirms `vertical: landscaping` and names the exact primary service.

## Common service families

- Landscape design and installation.
- Lawn care and maintenance.
- Sod installation and lawn renovation.
- Grading and yard drainage.
- Irrigation installation or repair.
- Landscape lighting.
- Planting, garden beds, trees, and shrubs.
- Seasonal cleanup when it is a real client service.

Do not assume the client offers every service. Keep tree removal, pest control, hardscaping, snow removal, and commercial maintenance separate unless the client approves them.

## High-intent research patterns

- `[service] company`
- `[service] contractor`
- `[service] near me`
- `[service] quote` or `[service] estimate`
- `[service] + city`
- `local [service]`

Use Keyword Planner or current account data for volume and CPC. Never invent metrics.

## Likely irrelevant intent to review

- DIY instructions, ideas, pictures, plans, software, free templates, jobs, salaries, courses, schools, supplies, equipment, and wholesale plants.
- Services the client does not provide.
- Locations outside the actual service area.

Do not add a negative until the full search term and client offer have been reviewed.

## Landing-page proof

Prefer verified project photos, service-specific reviews, before-and-after work, process, service area, scheduling expectations, and a short estimate form. Do not invent horticultural credentials, years in business, warranties, or response times.

# Sales Call Scorecard

Score each item 0, 1, or 2.

- Responded within the staffed-hours SLA.
- Confirmed the requested service and location.
- Asked about problem, urgency, timeline, and desired outcome.
- Qualified against the client's real service boundaries.
- Explained relevant verified value without overpromising.
- Addressed objections clearly.
- Secured a concrete next step.
- Logged disposition, follow-up owner, and due time.
- Preserved consent and opt-out preferences.
- Avoided false scarcity, pressure, or invented offers.

Record total score, outcome, coaching note, and next review date.

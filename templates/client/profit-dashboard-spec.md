# Profit Dashboard Specification

Required filters: date, client, campaign, ad group, service, location, lead type, device, day, and hour.

Required cards: spend, leads, qualified leads, sales, revenue, gross profit, profit after ads, CPL, CPQL, CAC, ROAS, gross-profit ROAS, close rate, attribution match rate, and median speed-to-lead.

Required tables:

- Campaign and keyword economics.
- Search terms ranked by spend and profit.
- Service/location closing and profit.
- Lead source and time-of-day performance.
- Unmatched spend, leads, and revenue.
- Recommendations ranked by estimated recoverable dollars.

Every metric must state its formula and data freshness.

# Review Collection Automation Template

Trigger only after a verified completed job.

1. Send one consent-compliant feedback request.
2. Collect a 1–5 rating and optional comments.
3. Store the response with job ID and timestamp.
4. Offer the same optional Google review link to every respondent.
5. If the score is low, create an additional internal recovery task without removing or hiding the review link.
6. Stop after completion or opt-out.
7. Do not offer incentives.
8. Report completion rate and review-link click rate as secondary analytics.

Required configuration: client, review URL, CRM/webhook, sender, consent text, delay after job completion, follow-up limit, opt-out handling, and service-recovery owner.
